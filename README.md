# HR-Attrition-Analytics-Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive, multi-page HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee turnover, workforce trends, and engagement metrics.

The dashboard transforms raw HR data into actionable insights to identify attrition drivers and improve employee retention strategies.
## Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Identify high-risk departments and roles
- Evaluate employee satisfaction & work-life balance
- Track new hiring trends
- Enable data-driven HR decisions

## Dataset Description

The dataset consists of rich employee-level attributes categorized as follows:

#### Demographic Information
- Age
- Gender
- Marital Status
- Age Band Classification (CF_age band)
- Education & Education Field
#### Job & Organizational Details
- Department (R&D, Sales, HR)
- Job Role & Job Level
- Business Travel
- Years at Company, Years in Current Role
- Years Since Last Promotion, Years with Current Manager
#### Compensation & Benefits
- Daily Rate, Monthly Income, Hourly Rate
- Percent Salary Hike
- Stock Option Level
#### Performance & Experience
- Performance Rating
- Total Working Years
- Number of Companies Worked
- Training Times Last Year
#### Satisfaction & Engagement Metrics
- Job Satisfaction
- Environment Satisfaction
- Relationship Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Job Involvement
#### Attrition Indicators
- Attrition (Yes/No)
- Attrition Label (CF_attrition label)
- Current Employee Status

## Dashboard Architecture

The dashboard is divided into multiple analytical pages, each focusing on a specific business perspective:

### 1. Attrition Overview
Provides a high-level snapshot of workforce status.
#### Key KPIs:
- Total Employees
- Current Employees
- Exiters
- Attrition Rate (%)
- Average Tenure
#### Visual Insights:
- Department-wise employee distribution
- Gender-based attrition comparison
- Overall workforce composition
  
### 2. Demographic Analysis
Analyzes how personal attributes influence attrition.
#### Key Visuals:
- Attrition by Age Group (Young, Mid, Senior)
- Gender-wise attrition trends
- Marital status vs attrition
#### Purpose:
- Identify vulnerable employee segments
- Understand demographic-driven turnover patterns

### 3. Role & Department Analysis
Focuses on organizational structure and job-related attrition.
#### Key Visuals:
- Attrition by Job Role
- Department-wise attrition comparison
- Role distribution across departments
#### Purpose:
- Detect high-risk roles
- Evaluate department-level retention issues

### 4. New Joiners Analysis
Tracks employee inflow and hiring trends.
#### Key Visuals:
- Total new joiners over time
- Education field distribution
-Gender-based hiring trends
- Prior experience (number of companies worked)
#### Purpose:
- Understand hiring patterns
- Evaluate workforce pipeline quality

## 5. Employee Experience & Satisfaction
Analyzes engagement and satisfaction factors affecting attrition.
#### Key Visuals:
- Job satisfaction vs attrition
- Work-life balance distribution
- Environment satisfaction levels
-Overtime vs attrition correlation
#### Purpose:
- Identify key satisfaction drivers
- Improve employee engagement strategies

## Tools 
- Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard creation & visualization
- Microsoft Excel – Data preparation & dataset handling
- Power Query – Data cleaning & transformation
- DAX – KPI calculations & measures

## Interactive Features
- Dynamic slicers (Department, Gender, Age Group, Job Role)
- Cross-filtering across visuals
- Drill-down capabilities for deeper insights
- Tooltip-based detailed analysis
- Multi-page navigation for structured exploration
